Where the side-panel popups open

The column type list opens with its left edge on its button and grows to the right, over the canvas, so the list stays in view. The description popover, Field Attributes, Index Attributes, Check Attributes and the table, type and note menus are anchored by their right edge and grow to the left, over the list; the colour picker is centred on its swatch. These cards compare ways to make them open the way the type list does. A was chosen for the first three; the other tabs show the same rule applied to the rest of the side panel.
