| table | bảng | 資料表 |

| diagram | sơ đồ | 圖表 |
| area | vùng | 區域 |
| note | ghi chú | 備註 |
| schema | lược đồ | 結構描述 |

| foreign key | khóa ngoại | 外部索引鍵 |

Register: vi neutral (no pronoun where possible, "bạn" otherwise); zh_TW 您.

Doubts: `check`, `enum`, `composite`, `ref`, `null`, `array` kept in English as SQL/DBML keywords. `Check` in the
zh_TW dialect messages kept as "check"; "Show standard/inline refs" keeps "ref". The vi "Số lượng" for cardinality
(cardinality) and zh_TW "基數" are the usual terms but may deserve a native check.
